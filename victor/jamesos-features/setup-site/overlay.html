JamesOS

Custom firmware for Anki Vector. Built on WireOS.

  • Holiday themes eyes, hats and back lights change with the holidays by themselves
  • Badges he unlocks achievements as you pet him and play
  • Sparkles and galaxy eyes little touches that make him feel alive
  • Rare surprises some days are special. Watch him.

Before you start

  • Vector must be unlocked (already running WireOS or another custom firmware).
  • Use Google Chrome on a computer with Bluetooth.
  • Vector on his charger, and this computer on the same Wi-Fi you'll give him.
