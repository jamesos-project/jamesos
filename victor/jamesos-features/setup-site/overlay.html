JamesOS

Install JamesOS on your Vector

Custom firmware built on WireOS. It takes about 10 minutes, and he restarts by himself when it's done.

Already on JamesOS? Just change his Wi-Fi connects him to a new network and skips the install.

Free software under the GPL-3.0. Source code

What you need

  • A Vector that's already unlocked, running WireOS or another custom firmware
  • Google Chrome on a computer with Bluetooth
  • Vector on his charger, and this computer on the Wi-Fi you'll give him
  • Holiday themesHis eyes and back lights change with the holidays.
  • BadgesHe unlocks achievements as you pet and play with him.
  • SparklesGalaxy eyes, stars and rare surprise days.
  • Phone pageChange his settings from your phone.
JamesOS

Wi-Fi changed

He's on the new Wi-Fi. Nothing else was changed.

For voice commands, wire-pod has to be on the same Wi-Fi as him.
